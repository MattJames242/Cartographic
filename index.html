How to play

The map shows where a famous person was born and where they died, with the years.

Type your guess. You get 4 tries per person. Points: 4 → 3 → 2 → 1.

You only have 3 hints for the whole game, so use them wisely. Each hint on a person reveals the next clue:

  1. Place names
  2. Who they were
  3. Their initials
